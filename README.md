# 🦄 Save the Unicorns

*Use the available hats and items to save the unicorns lives!🎩*

You run an ordinary hat stall in an ordinary town — except some of your customers aren't ordinary at all. Hunted unicorns come to you cloaked in secrecy, and it's up to you to turn them into "another horse" before they go to the checkpoint and hopefully cross the borders.

## The Game

- **Town Scene** — the story unfolds. Who's arrived today, and what do they need from you?
- **Disguise the Unicorn** — dress them down, hide the horn, pick the mane dye, choose the coat color. Get it wrong and it could cost them everything.
- **The Crossing** — hold your breath as they walk the checkpoint. If they survive they might send you some gold, use it wisely.

## How To Play?

Open `save-the-unicorns.html` in your browser. That's it.

*The border only gets more careful. So should you.*
